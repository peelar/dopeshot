# dopeshot — Vision

## Purpose

Be the fastest way for indie hackers and small builders to turn their product into a cohesive, postable visual identity.

## Principles

- Speed first: upload to export in seconds; no tutorials or onboarding gates.
- Smart defaults: everything looks good out of the box; edits are optional, not required.
- Consistent voice: outputs share the same energy so users build a recognizable look.
- Repeat use: it should feel faster and more familiar every time someone returns.

## Differentiation

- Start from user content, not a blank canvas.
- Offer a few curated choices instead of dozens of knobs.
- Automatic color extraction and palette matching; layouts show structure, effects add polish.
- Clear mental model: layouts (geometry), effects (treatments), backgrounds (surfaces).

## Scope guardrails

- Focus now: product screenshots to Twitter/LinkedIn-friendly graphics.
- Architecture: clean separation of layouts (geometry), effects (treatments), backgrounds (surfaces).
- Next surfaces (after validation): headers and content covers; brand integration via background tokens.
- Avoid enterprise workflows and heavy customization that slow people down.

## Success measures

- Time: upload to export stays under 30s end-to-end.
- Quality: output is "post without hesitation" for the vast majority of uploads.
- Retention: the same users come back weekly with minimal friction.
- Cohesion: posts look like the same brand across formats.

## Voice

Confident, supportive, a little irreverent. Direct copy; no corporate fluff. We celebrate people shipping quickly.
