# Copywriting Formulas for Landing Pages

## PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution)

**Structure:**
1. **Problem**: Identify the pain point
2. **Agitate**: Make it hurt more (consequences, frustration)
3. **Solution**: Introduce your product as the relief

**Example:**
- **Problem**: "Spending hours manually processing customer support tickets?"
- **Agitate**: "Every minute wasted is a frustrated customer waiting, revenue slipping away, and your team burning out on repetitive work."
- **Solution**: "AutoSupport handles 80% of tickets instantly, so your team focuses on complex issues that matter."

---

## AIDA (Attention-Interest-Desire-Action)

**Structure:**
1. **Attention**: Grab focus with bold claim or question
2. **Interest**: Build curiosity with specifics
3. **Desire**: Create emotional want with benefits
4. **Action**: Clear CTA with urgency

**Example:**
- **Attention**: "What if your sales team closed 3x more deals?"
- **Interest**: "Most sales teams waste 60% of their time on admin work instead of selling."
- **Desire**: "Imagine your reps spending entire days in conversations, not CRMs. More pipeline, faster closes, happier sellers."
- **Action**: "Start your 14-day trial - no credit card required"

---

## Before/After/Bridge

**Structure:**
1. **Before**: Current painful state
2. **After**: Ideal future state
3. **Bridge**: Your product is the path

**Example:**
- **Before**: "Right now, you're juggling 6 different tools just to ship one feature."
- **After**: "Imagine a single platform where code, design, and deployment flow seamlessly."
- **Bridge**: "ShipFast connects your entire workflow, so you ship in hours, not weeks."

---

## Feature → Advantage → Benefit (FAB)

**Translation table:**

| Feature | Advantage | Benefit |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| AI-powered analytics | Surfaces insights automatically | Spend time acting on data, not finding it |
| Real-time collaboration | Everyone sees changes instantly | No more version conflicts or wasted meetings |
| One-click deployment | Push to production in seconds | Ship features while competitors are still testing |
| Template library | 100+ pre-built components | Launch your MVP this week, not next quarter |

Always lead with the benefit, not the feature.

## The 4 U's Headline Formula

Great headlines are:
1. **Useful**: Delivers clear value
2. **Urgent**: Creates time pressure
3. **Unique**: Stands out from competition
4. **Ultra-specific**: Uses numbers and details

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Better project management"
- ✅ "Cut project delivery time by 40% in the next 30 days"

- ❌ "Grow your audience"
- ✅ "Gain your first 10,000 email subscribers in 90 days"

**Specific Outcomes:**

**Authority Signals:**
- "Featured in TechCrunch, WSJ, and Forbes"
- "SOC 2 Type II Certified • GDPR Compliant • ISO 27001"

## Objection Handling Copy

Address common objections before the CTA:

**"Too expensive"**
→ "Most customers see ROI within 60 days"
→ "Start free, upgrade when you see value"

**"Too complex to implement"**
→ "Live in 5 minutes with our guided setup"
→ "Import your data automatically - no manual work"

**"What if it doesn't work for us?"**
→ "30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked"
→ "Cancel anytime with one click"

**"We already have a solution"**
→ "Integrates with [competitor] - try both simultaneously"
→ "See the difference in a side-by-side demo"

## Power Words for Conversion

**Speed & Efficiency:**
instant, immediately, fast, rapid, quick, automated, streamlined

**Ease & Simplicity:**
effortless, simple, easy, seamless, intuitive, one-click, automatic

**Transformation:**
transform, revolutionize, breakthrough, game-changer, unlock, unleash

**Results & Proof:**
proven, guaranteed, tested, validated, certified, trusted, verified

**Exclusivity:**
exclusive, limited, secret, insider, private, members-only

**Emotion & Impact:**
powerful, stunning, amazing, remarkable, extraordinary, breakthrough

Avoid: synergy, leverage, robust, cutting-edge, innovative, revolutionary (overused)
