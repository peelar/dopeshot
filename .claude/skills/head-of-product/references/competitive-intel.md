# Competitive Intelligence

## Direct Competitors

### Screenshot Beautifiers
- **Screely** — simple, one-shot, browser mockups. No brand persistence.
- **Pika** — screenshot to polished graphic. Clean UX. No brand system.
- **Screen.studio** — more video-focused but screenshot mode exists.
- **Cleanshot X** — Mac screenshot tool with annotation. Not a finishing tool.
- **Shots.so** — mockup generator. More design-heavy, slower.

### Gradient / Background Beautifiers
- **InstantGradient** (instantgradient.com) — "Your screenshots, but sexier." Gradient backgrounds for screenshots, GIFs, and videos. Supports MP4/GIF export, platform-ready sizes (IG Stories, Twitter headers, TikTok). Freemium model. Built by @ugo_builds, appears actively maintained. Closest competitor in spirit — fast, focused, indie-built. But gradient-only (no layouts, no brand system, no effects beyond background). Good at the one thing it does.

### What They All Have in Common
- One-shot tools: upload -> tweak -> download
- No brand persistence across sessions
- No concept of "your brand defaults"
- Often designed for designers, not builders

### Where DopeShot Wins
- **Brand consistency** as a first-class feature (not just pretty one-offs)
- **Repeat use** by design (same brand every time, no re-configuring)
- **Speed** — fewer choices, faster flow
- **Indie hacker focus** — built for the workflow of people who ship and post

### Where DopeShot Loses (For Now)
- Fewer templates/layouts than mature tools
- No batch processing
- Desktop-only
- No video/GIF support

## Adjacent Tools (Not Direct Competitors)

### Social Media Tools

- **Typefully/Hypefury** — Twitter writing tools, no visual component

### AI Image Tools
- **Midjourney/DALL-E** — generate from scratch, not finish existing screenshots

## Competitive Positioning

DopeShot doesn't compete on features or template count. It competes on:

1. **Speed of the core flow** — fastest path from screenshot to share-ready
2. **Brand consistency** — only tool that bakes your brand into every export
3. **Repeat-use design** — gets faster the more you use it
4. **Indie hacker fit** — built for the exact workflow of builders who post

**The moat is brand lock-in**: Once your brand tokens are dialed in, switching to another tool means losing that consistency. Every export reinforces the habit.

---

## What NOT to Copy From Competitors

- Template galleries with 500+ options (decision fatigue)
- Drag-and-drop canvases (too slow, wrong mental model)
- Enterprise features (teams, approval workflows, RBAC)
- Integration marketplaces (premature at this stage)
- Free tier that's crippled (ours should be genuinely useful)
