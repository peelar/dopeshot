# DOPE SHOT - PRODUCT VISION

**Last Updated:** November 2025  
**Status:** Active Development

---

## WHAT WE'RE BUILDING

Dope Shot is the visual identity toolkit for indie hackers and small builders.

We help people who are building products and building in public create professional graphics for their online presence—fast.

---

## WHO THIS IS FOR

**Primary User: The Indie Hacker / Small Builder**

- Building a product (solo or small team)
- Building in public on Twitter, LinkedIn, newsletters
- Posting frequently about progress, launches, features, milestones
- Needs professional-looking graphics but doesn't have time or design skills
- Values speed and consistency over endless customization
- Willing to pay for tools that save time and look good

**Not for:**

- Large companies with design teams
- People who want full design control (use Figma/Canva)
- One-time users (we optimize for repeat use)

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### Color Intelligence

- Extract dominant and accent colors from uploaded content
- Generate complementary gradients automatically
- Adjust text colors for readability
- Make gradients lighter where text appears
- Everything matches the user's content vibe

**User doesn't think about color theory. It just works.**

### Typography System

Users choose fonts by **vibe**, not font name:

- **Founder Mode** - Clean, modern (default)
- **Corporate** - Professional, serious
- **Terminal** - Developer tools vibe
- **Unhinged** - Bold, geometric, indie hacker
- **Normie** - Friendly, approachable
- **Chaotic** - Edgy, design-forward
- **Spreadsheet** - Technical, data/analytics
- **Expensive** - Premium, polished

Philosophy: Every choice looks good. Pick based on mood, not typography knowledge.

### Template Philosophy

- Automatically suggest best template based on content
- Minimal variants (3-5 per template)
- Each variant clearly different (not subtle variations)
- User can switch with one click
- Most users never need to customize
