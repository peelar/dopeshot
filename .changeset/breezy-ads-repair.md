Replace branded gradient slots 4-6 with distinctive visual effects for better variety

Brand users now get three unique gradient styles instead of similar-looking yellow variations:
- **Aurora Dream** (slot 4): Ethereal multi-radial elliptical gradients with soft overlapping color zones
- **Morning Haze** (slot 5): Bottom-centered concentric radial gradients creating a sunrise/glow effect  
- **Lavender Blush Flow** (slot 6): Sophisticated 6-stop linear gradient with smooth analogous color transitions

These effects work in both light and dark brand modes, adapting their color palettes accordingly while maintaining the brand accent as the anchor color.
