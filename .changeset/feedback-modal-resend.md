Add feedback submission feature with email delivery via Resend. Users can now submit feedback directly from the app through a new "Feedback" button in the top navigation (available on both desktop and mobile). The feedback modal allows users to share their thoughts, include an optional email for follow-up, and attach a screenshot of their current canvas. All feedback is delivered to the builder's inbox via Resend email service with rich context including user info, page URL, timestamp, and browser details.
